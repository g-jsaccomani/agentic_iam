## 3. Graph-Theoretic Escalation Mechanics & Toxic Permission Combinations

A widespread blind spot in enterprise IAM reviews is treating cloud permissions as a flat table of `(principal, role, project)` rows. In Google Cloud, identity privileges form a **directed graph** $G = (V, E)$ where principals can mint short-lived tokens for, attach workloads to, or mutate policies of intermediate identities — creating transitive administrative blast radii even when the entry identity holds zero direct roles on the target crown-jewel project.

- **Betweenness Centrality & Choke-Point Mathematics (`nx.betweenness_centrality`):** For every identity or workload node $v \in V$, `backend/tools/graph_engine.py` computes shortest-path betweenness centrality $C_B(v) = \sum_{s \neq v \neq t} \frac{\sigma_{st}(v)}{\sigma_{st}}$ alongside normalized degree centrality $C_D(v)$ into a combined choke-point score (`0.6 * C_B + 0.4 * C_D`). Identities with high $C_B(v)$ (such as shared CI/CD deployment Service Accounts or Default Compute SAs) act as **bridges** connecting dozens of developers or external pipelines to production data stores; remediating a single high-centrality node severs multiple escalation chains simultaneously.
- **Transitive Service Account Impersonation & Execution Vectors (`SA_IMPERSONATION_CHAIN` & `GCE_METADATA_ESCALATION`):** Any principal granted `roles/iam.serviceAccountTokenCreator` (`iam.serviceAccounts.getAccessToken` / `signBlob`), `roles/iam.serviceAccountKeyAdmin` (`iam.serviceAccountKeys.create`), or `roles/iam.serviceAccountUser` (`iam.serviceAccounts.actAs`) paired with workload-mutation roles (`roles/compute.instanceAdmin.v1`, `roles/run.admin`, `roles/cloudfunctions.developer`, `roles/cloudbuild.builds.editor`) inherits the **full downstream privilege set** of every reachable Service Account in that scope.
- **Custom Role Privilege Injection & Separation-of-Duties Violations (`iam.roles.update` & KMS/Logging SoD):** Custom Roles containing `iam.roles.update` allow a holder to inject arbitrary permissions (such as `resourcemanager.projects.setIamPolicy`) into their own assigned role. Similarly, granting `roles/cloudkms.admin` + `roles/cloudkms.cryptoKeyEncrypterDecrypter` on the same identity violates cryptographic split-knowledge, while combining `roles/logging.configWriter` (`logging.exclusions.create` / `logging.sinks.delete`) with `roles/storage.admin` or `roles/bigquery.admin` allows silent audit-log suppression during data exfiltration.
- **Unconditioned Workload Identity Federation (`ORG_POLICY_BYPASS` / OIDC Repo Takeover):** Configuring an external OIDC `WorkloadIdentityPoolProvider` (e.g., `https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com`) without a strict CEL `attributeCondition` binding `assertion.repository_owner_id` allows **any external public repository** on that issuer to exchange OIDC tokens for `roles/iam.workloadIdentityUser` if subject mappings are overly broad.
- **Project Convenience Member Inheritance (`projectOwner:<pid>` / `projectEditor:<pid>`):** Legacy BigQuery datasets and Cloud Storage buckets frequently retain `projectEditor:<project_id>` ACL entries, silently granting full data read/write access to every principal holding primitive `roles/editor` at the project or parent folder level.

## 4. Standing-Privilege Exposure Window & Optional Business-Impact Sizing

To help CISOs, Chief Risk Officers, and CFOs prioritize remediation waves, the Agentic IAM engagement pairs empirical permission-reduction math with optional, customer-calibrated risk sizing:

- **Permission Attack-Surface Reduction Ratio (`permission_reduction_pct`):** For every over-privileged binding, `recommender_tool.py` queries the live IAM Roles API (`iam.googleapis.com/v1/roles/*`) for exact `permissions_granted_count` ($G$) and correlates bounded Cloud Audit Log samples (`logging.googleapis.com/v2/entries:list`, reporting `is_sample`, `sample_size`, and observed window) plus IAM Recommender insights for observed exercised permissions ($U$), computing auditable surface reduction $\Delta P = \max(0, G - U)$ and $\text{Reduction \%} = \frac{G - U}{\max(G, 1)} \times 100$. Replacing a single `roles/editor` binding ($\sim 8,000+$ permissions) on a workload exercising 14 storage/logging permissions eliminates $>99.8\%$ of standing permission surface (teams then validate with Policy Simulator before applying).
- **Standing Exposure Window ($T_{\text{standing}} = 8,760\text{ hrs/yr}$ vs. $T_{\text{PAM-JIT}}$):** Converting a permanent human `Owner` grant ($8,760$ exposed hours per year) into a 1-hour **Privileged Access Manager (PAM)** entitlement invoked twice per month ($24$ audited hours per year) reduces temporal credential exposure by **99.73%**.
- **Business-Impact Sizing (Optional, Customer-Provided Inputs Only):** Consistent with strict anti-hallucination governance, the Agentic IAM tools and Gemini agents **never invent dollar figures**. When requested during the Week 4 Risk Workshop, PSO Consultants combine the crown-jewel BigQuery/GCS/KMS assets reachable via discovered NetworkX attack paths with the customer's own data-classification volumes, historical incident costs, cyber-insurance deductibles/BIA, and statutory fine schedules (e.g., LGPD, GDPR, PCI-DSS, DORA), presenting financial exposure strictly as transparent ranges with documented assumptions.

## 6. Domain-by-Domain Prescriptive Security Recommendations (Domains 1 to 7)

- **Domain 1 (Workforce Federation, SSO/MFA & JML Lifecycle):** Federate corporate workforce identities from the authoritative HR-linked IdP (Cloud Identity / Workspace, Entra ID, Okta, or Ping) via SAML 2.0 / OIDC with automated SCIM user lifecycle provisioning and immediate suspension propagation; enforce phishing-resistant FIDO2/Passkey 2SV on all administrative and engineering personas; and audit/revoke IAM bindings belonging to suspended or departed accounts.
- **Domain 2 (Group-Based RBAC & Cloud Identity Security Group Hygiene):** Prohibit direct `user:` bindings in Organization, Folder, and Project IAM policies (`100%` Group RBAC target); convert all IAM-bound groups to locked **Security Groups** (`cloudidentity.googleapis.com/groups.security`), assign at least two active `OWNER`/`MANAGER` custodians per group, flatten transitive group nesting (`<= 2` levels), and block external member additions on internal RBAC groups.
- **Domain 3 (Privileged Access Manager PAM JIT & Break-Glass Governance):** Migrate all standing human `roles/owner`, `roles/editor`, `roles/resourcemanager.organizationAdmin`, `roles/resourcemanager.folderAdmin`, and `roles/iam.securityAdmin` grants into **PAM Entitlements** (`3600s` max duration, peer/SecOps approval, ticket reference required); maintain at most two hardware-key-protected emergency break-glass accounts excluded from third-party IdP outages and wired to real-time Cloud Monitoring / SIEM high-severity alerts on any sign-in event.
- **Domain 4 (Workload Identity Federation & Static SA Key Retirement):** Enforce `constraints/iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation` and `constraints/iam.disableServiceAccountKeyUpload` across production folders; migrate GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, AWS/Azure, and on-premises workloads to **Workload Identity Federation** with explicit `attributeCondition` pinning organization/repository IDs; rotate and delete all legacy user-managed Service Account keys older than 90 days after verifying `lastAuthenticatedTime` in Policy Analyzer / Cloud Audit Logs; and enforce `constraints/iam.automaticIamGrantsForDefaultServiceAccounts`.
- **Domain 5 (Impersonation Chain Severing, Custom Roles & Separation of Duties):** Never bind `roles/iam.serviceAccountTokenCreator` or `roles/iam.serviceAccountUser` at the Organization, Folder, or Project root — bind them exclusively on the specific target `google_service_account` resource; remove `iam.roles.update` from operational custom roles; and enforce strict Separation of Duties between `roles/cloudkms.admin` and `roles/cloudkms.cryptoKeyEncrypterDecrypter`, as well as between `roles/logging.configWriter` and data-plane administrative roles.
- **Domain 6 (External/Partner Sharing DRS & VPC Service Controls):** Enforce `constraints/iam.allowedPolicyMemberDomains` restricted to the customer's verified Cloud Identity Customer ID (`C0...`), handling approved B2B partner access exclusively via dedicated partner folders or time-bound IAM Conditions (`request.time < timestamp(...)`); enforce `constraints/storage.publicAccessPrevention` Organization-wide; and enclose Tier-0 BigQuery, Cloud Storage, Secret Manager, and Cloud KMS resources within **VPC Service Controls (VPC-SC)** perimeters.
- **Domain 7 (IAM-as-Code CI/CD, Policy Simulator Gates & Drift Detection):** Manage 100% of Organization, Folder, Project, Custom Role, PAM Entitlement, and Org Policy bindings declaratively via OpenTofu/Terraform (`google_project_iam_member` / authoritative `google_project_iam_binding`), run automated **Policy Simulator** checks on pull requests modifying IAM modules, and enable Security Command Center (SCC) CIEM / Cloud Asset Feeds to alert on out-of-band console IAM mutations.

## 7. Preventive Governance Advisory: Declarative GCP Organization Policy Reference Templates

Remediating IAM drift without preventive guardrails allows new projects and pipelines to reintroduce static keys and public exposure within days. Google Cloud PSO delivers five declarative GCP Organization Policy reference controls in `org_policies.tf` (for the Customer's platform governance team to validate with Policy Simulator and roll out via CI/CD):

1. **Block New Static Service Account JSON Keys (`constraints/iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation` & `disableServiceAccountKeyUpload`):** Enforces keyless authentication across workloads by blocking creation and external public-key upload of user-managed Service Account keys (with scoped folder/tag exceptions only for documented legacy appliances during transition).
2. **Eliminate Automatic `Editor` on Default Service Accounts (`constraints/iam.automaticIamGrantsForDefaultServiceAccounts`):** Prevents newly created projects from automatically granting primitive `roles/editor` to `<project-number>-compute@developer.gserviceaccount.com` and `<project-id>@appspot.gserviceaccount.com`.
3. **Enforce Domain Restricted Sharing (`constraints/iam.allowedPolicyMemberDomains`):** Restricts IAM policy members strictly to the Customer's verified Cloud Identity `directoryCustomerId`, automatically blocking accidental `@gmail.com`, unapproved partner domain, `allUsers`, and `allAuthenticatedUsers` grants.
4. **Enforce Cloud Storage Public Access Prevention (`constraints/storage.publicAccessPrevention`):** Guarantees no bucket or object across the Organization can be exposed to `allUsers` or `allAuthenticatedUsers` even if a principal holds `roles/storage.admin`.
5. **Enforce OS Login for Compute SSH (`constraints/compute.requireOsLogin`):** Replaces static project-wide SSH metadata keys (a primary `GCE_METADATA_ESCALATION` vector) with IAM-governed, 2SV-capable OS Login roles (`roles/compute.osLogin` / `osAdminLogin`).

### 9.3 Advisory Per-Finding Reference Guidelines (For Future Customer-Led CI/CD Adoption)

> **Advisory Scope Notice:** Google Cloud Consulting (PSO) does not execute IAM mutations, key deletions, or Terraform applies in the Customer's environment. The reference steps below illustrate the consultative engineering guidelines generated alongside `agentic_iam_terraform_remediation_bundle.zip` for review and execution by the Customer's platform team under CAB governance.

#### 9.3.1 Advisory Reference 1 — Standing Human `roles/owner` -> Privileged Access Manager (PAM) 1-Hour JIT Entitlement

1. **Reference Step 1 — Provision Locked Cloud Identity Approver Security Group:** `gcloud identity groups create secops-pam-approvers@<CUSTOMER-DOMAIN> --organization=<CUSTOMER-ORG-ID> --group-type=security --display-name="SecOps PAM JIT Approvers"`
2. **Reference Step 2 — Deploy PAM Entitlement (`pam_entitlements.tf`):** Configure `google_privileged_access_manager_entitlement` on the target project/folder with `max_request_duration = "3600s"`, `manual_approvals` requiring `group:secops-pam-approvers@<CUSTOMER-DOMAIN>`, and `unstructured` justification mandatory.
3. **Reference Step 3 — Verify Policy Simulator & Break-Glass Readiness:** Confirm emergency break-glass accounts are tested and monitored in Cloud Logging before modifying standing human bindings.
4. **Reference Step 4 — Transition Standing `user:` Primitive Binding:** Grant baseline read-only visibility (`roles/viewer`) via a Cloud Identity Security Group and remove the standing `user:<ADMIN>@<CUSTOMER-DOMAIN>` `roles/owner` binding during a scheduled change window.
5. **Reference Step 5 — Verification & Rollback Reference:** Test a 30-minute PAM JIT elevation request via `gcloud beta pam grants create` and retain the previous IAM policy ETag snapshot in Git state for instant rollback under CAB governance.

#### 9.3.2 Advisory Reference 2 — Static Service Account JSON Key (`>90d`) -> Keyless Workload Identity Federation (WIF)

1. **Reference Step 1 — Create Workload Identity Pool & Hardened OIDC Provider (`iam.tf`):** `gcloud iam workload-identity-pools create github-cicd-pool --location=global --project=<TARGET-PROJECT-ID>` followed by `create-oidc` with `--issuer-uri="https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com"` and `--attribute-condition="assertion.repository_owner_id == '<VERIFIED-ORG-ID>'"`.
2. **Reference Step 2 — Bind Dedicated Least-Privilege SA Only to Specific Repository Subject:** Bind `roles/iam.workloadIdentityUser` on the dedicated workload Service Account exclusively for `principalSet://iam.googleapis.com/projects/<PROJECT-NUMBER>/locations/global/workloadIdentityPools/github-cicd-pool/attribute.repository/<ORG>/<REPO>`.
3. **Reference Step 3 — Right-Size Workload Role via 90-Day Audit Replay:** Replace `roles/editor` on the workload SA with the Policy Simulator-verified Custom Role (`iam.tf`) containing only the permissions exercised in Cloud Audit Logs.
4. **Reference Step 4 — Cut Over Pipeline to `google-github-actions/auth@v2` & Disable Legacy Key:** Switch the CI/CD workflow to OIDC token exchange, disable the legacy static key via `gcloud iam service-accounts keys disable <KEY-ID>`, and monitor Policy Analyzer `lastAuthenticatedTime` for 7 days.
5. **Reference Step 5 — Delete Disabled Key & Enforce Preventive Org Policy:** Delete the disabled static key and enforce `constraints/iam.disableServiceAccountKeyCreation` in `org_policies.tf` to prevent future key regressions.
